## §1. App Store Connect Reply (영문)

**Where:** App Store Connect → 해당 버전 → Resolution Center → **Reply**

```
Subject: Response to Guideline 4.3(a) — Keyring 1.0 (build 34+)

Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for your follow-up review. We understand Guideline 4.3(a) and have made substantive product and store listing changes since build 32. Keyring is an original app developed independently by our team — not a repackaged template, duplicate binary, or submission under another account.

1) What Keyring is (distinct concept)

Keyring is a routine checklist app where selected apps and websites stay blocked until the user finishes today’s routine tasks — not a fixed-duration timer blocker or generic “focus” app.

• Lock is tied to task completion: restrictions lift only when all items in the active routine are checked off on the Today tab.
• Quick Lock: block apps for N minutes without creating a routine (secondary mode, clearly separate in the UI).
• Routines include schedules, rest days, deadline extension, history/streaks, widgets, and App Intents — all implemented in our own SwiftUI codebase.

2) Independent development

• Built in-house: Swift/SwiftUI, SwiftData, custom Family Controls / Device Activity extensions, widgets, Live Activity, and App Intents.
• We have not purchased, licensed, or submitted any third-party app-blocker or Screen Time reskin/template.
• This is our first App Store app on this developer account. We have not published a similar app under another bundle ID or Apple Developer account.

3) iPhone-only app — please review on iPhone

Our app target is iPhone only (UIDeviceFamily = iPhone). We have removed iPad screenshots and previews from App Store Connect for this version.

If the review was performed on iPad, the experience may appear as a scaled phone UI and could look similar to other generic blockers. We respectfully ask that the core flow be evaluated on an iPhone:

Guide → Routine (tasks + apps to block) → allow Screen Time → Today (check off tasks) → lock releases when complete.

4) Changes since the previous rejection (build 32 → 34+)

• Clearer onboarding and Today-tab hero: “Complete tasks → unlock apps”
• Quick Lock surfaced on the Today tab
• Free tier limits enforced in-app (routines, items, apps/websites) with a one-time IAP (no subscription)
• 14-day full-feature trial for new users, then free tier + optional lifetime unlock (₩7,900)
• Updated App Store screenshots and metadata focused on checklist-based unlocking (not generic “habit” or “focus timer” positioning)

5) Request for clarification

To address any remaining concern precisely, could you share which existing App Store app(s) Keyring was compared to (name or category)? We are happy to adjust metadata or highlight specific differentiators if something still appears overlapping.

Review notes and a step-by-step test path are in App Review Information → Notes.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
[Your name]
[Apple Developer account name]
[Contact email — same as App Review contact]
```

## §7. Review Board (3회 거절 시)

**언제:** 동일 Guideline 4.3(a)로 **3번째** 거절, 구체적 비교 앱명 없음, 위 변경을 모두 반영했는데도 “similar binary/metadata”만 반복될 때.

**절차**

1. [App Review Board](https://developer.apple.com/contact/app-store/?topic=appeal) → **Appeal** 선택  
2. Submission ID·버전·빌드 번호 명시  
3. §1 영문 Reply 핵심 + “iPhone-only, independent development, checklist-unlock concept” 재강조  
4. Connect Reply 스크린샷·변경 diff(스크린샷 before/after) 첨부 가능하면 첨부  

**Appeal 초안 (영문)**

```
We are appealing Guideline 4.3(a) rejection for Keyring 1.0 (build XX).

Keyring is independently developed (Swift/SwiftUI, custom Screen Time extensions). Its core mechanic — apps stay blocked until the user completes a routine checklist — is not a repackaged timer blocker. We are iPhone-only, removed iPad assets, updated metadata and screenshots, and documented a clear review path in Review Notes.

We have not used third-party templates or published similar apps on other accounts. Without knowing which App Store app Keyring was compared to, we cannot address a specific overlap. We request App Review Board consideration or clarification of the comparable app(s).

Submission ID: [ID]
Contact: [email]
```
